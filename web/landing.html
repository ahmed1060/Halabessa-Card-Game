Halabessa is an Egyptian card game, also known as حلبسة or حلبسه. Play online, meet your friends at the table, or practice against bots.

You may also know Halabessa as 7alabessa, halabesa, or 7alabesa — they all refer to the same game.

The game supports Arabic and English, with a shared tabletop experience on mobile and desktop browsers. No app download is needed to play on the web.
