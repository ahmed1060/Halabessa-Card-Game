# Halabessa Lantern Nights logo

`halabessa-lantern-icon-v1.png` is the approved raster master, created with built-in image generation. Its exports are installed in the Android launcher, iOS Runner AppIcon catalog, web manifest, browser favicon, and Apple touch icon. The original master is preserved.

Run `powershell -File scripts/export-brand-icons.ps1` on Windows to reproduce all exports, or add `-Check` to verify their sizes and absence of alpha. Native iOS exports include the opaque 1024px marketing icon. Android API 26+ uses an adaptive icon, with additional safe-zone padding; web maskable icons also have additional padding. Standard icons retain the approved composition. Square corners are deliberate: the platform applies its own mask.

The text-free lantern and playing-card emblem uses the approved plum, amber, cream, mint, and coral palette. The game name remains **حلبسه / Halabessa** in surrounding UI.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a single production-ready square app icon for Halabessa (Egyptian card game), matching its approved Lantern Nights arcade cafe visual theme. 1024x1024 composition. Full bleed opaque solid deep plum #3B274C background, square corners (platform applies corner masks). Center one bold original emblem: a compact fan of three chunky cream playing cards, with a warm amber Egyptian cafe lantern integrated as the central card's oversized suit symbol. Two rear cards have small simple mint and coral suit motifs. Lantern has a clean domed cap, handle, and one diamond-shaped warm light window; instantly recognizable, simplified, not intricate. Friendly illustrated arcade identity, crisp vector-like shapes, thick dark ink #192638 outlines, cream #FFF0D1, amber #FFC65B, mint #78D2AF, tiny coral #ED767A accent. Front card upright, rear cards gently angled. Strong silhouette, balanced negative space, modest two-tone flat shading for playful depth, extremely clean edges. Entire emblem inside central 66% of square for safe circular and squircle cropping. No outer border, no separate badge, no thin filigree, no small pips or numbers, no lettering, no text whatsoever, no watermark, no photo, no neon, no casino chips, no mockup, no background scene. Output only the actual finished icon artwork, not a presentation sheet. Designed to remain identifiable as a 32-pixel favicon while also looking polished as a mobile app icon.
